
# Simple Distance between two points is absolute value

Intuition is telling you to do it like this **but don't follow your intuition for this one**:
$$
\begin{gather}
distance = 9 - 5
\\
distance = 4
\end{gather}
$$

You should understand that distance is always positive value; **distance is absolute value**.

![[Simple Distance.excalidraw|700]]

So you can use `abs` (Rember `Math.abs` in javascript)

$$
\begin{gather}
distance = abs(5 - 9) \\

distance = abs(-4) \\
distance = 4
\end{gather}

$$
Order isn't important since you are uing abs.

Understanding how to calculate distance on the number line allows us to determine spatial relationships, mesure intervals and analyze positons effectivly.

Foundational knowladge to have before you hit multi dimensional speaces.
