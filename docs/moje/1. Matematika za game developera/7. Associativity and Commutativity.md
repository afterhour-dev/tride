
Vector addition is both
- Addition is Associative
	- Grouping of vectors doesn't affect result
	- If you have 3 vectors, result will be the same, no matter how they're grouped
	- $$
          (\vec{a} + \vec{b}) + \vec{c} = \vec{a} + (\vec{b} + \vec{c})
	   $$
- Addition is Commutative
	- Order doesn't affect the result
	- Given two vectors, result will be the same, no matter ho they are ordered
	-  $$
          \vec{a} + \vec{b} = \vec{b} + \vec{a}
	   $$
- Subtraction is **NOT** associative or commutative
