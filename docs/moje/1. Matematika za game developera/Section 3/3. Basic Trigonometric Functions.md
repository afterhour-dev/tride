# General definition

we are looking triangle with adjecent (also called base) (nalaegla) side, corresponding to x axes; and we have oposite (naspramna) side, corresponding to y axis. We also have hypotenuse corresponding to the unit Vector.

![[2026-09-22_19-53.png]]

If we put our triagle into unit circle (or imagine we put it) we can see that hypotenuse is 1, and we are deviding adjecent with 1 to get cosinus and deviding opposite by 1 to get sinus, meaning sinus will be same as adjecent side length and cosinus will be same as oposite side length.

# Oscilations

Let's grow angle on a unit circle from 0 to 2PI and beyond 

![[2026-09-22_20-57.png]]

![[2026-09-22_20-42.png]]

We ploted sin and cos on the unit circle, and  as you following circle you get oscilations, you get exact same curve for both, but with slight offset.

**Offset is exaclly `PI / 2` radians or 90deg**

So this is true

`sin(theta) = cos(theta - PI/2)

You can notice this easily when angle is 90deg or PI/2 radians; at that angle; there you have 

`sin(PI/2) === cos(PI/2 - PI/2)`  ;      `sin(PI/2) === cos(0)`     ;     1 === 1 

**So they are same graph but shifterd by PI/2 radians**
