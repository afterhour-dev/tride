
What is the dot product also called `Scalar Product` or `Scalar Projection` but almost everyone is using term `Dot Product` ?
- Takes two vectors and produces a scalar
- mesures similarity of 2 vectors
	- how closely they align in direction

## How to compute dot product

we have two vectors `a` and `b`
`dot(a,b) = sclarValue`

`scalarValue` is floating point value

![[ditproduct.excalidraw|700]]

But what is the result scalar actually representing; this is geometric representation
$$
\begin{gather}
\vec{v}_{1} \cdot \vec{v_{2}} = |\lvert a\rvert| & |\lvert b\rvert| & \cos{\theta} 
\end{gather}
$$
`||a||` is magnitude/length of a vector, `||b||`  is magnitude/length og b vector

And theta angle is angle between a vector and b vector

So you can also calculate dot product the other way; if you have angel between two vectors, you can multiply cosinus of the angle and  magnitudes/lengths of the vectors

## What a dot product gives you

Dot product is related to the cosine of the angl between two vectors, scaled by the magnitude of both vectors
