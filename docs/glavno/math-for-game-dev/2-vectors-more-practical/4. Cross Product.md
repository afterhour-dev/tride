# Determinants

We should get familiar with determinate. I forgot how to use them because high school was long time ago.

A **determinant** is a special number calculated from a **square matrix**. Square matrix is matrix with same amount of rows and columns.

We will cover math behind matrices especially: [[2. Basic Matrix Math]], but that doesn't stop us from using determinants here.

I won't get into explaining how and why they work, this is for you to do if you are curious. I'm just going to show how we calculate them.

You multiply each top element by the 2x2 determinant that remains if you cross out that element's row and column. Remember to alternate the signs (+,-,+): 

$$
\begin{vmatrix} \color{red}{a} & \color{blue}{b} & \color{green}{c} \\ d & e & f \\ g & h & i \end{vmatrix} = \color{red}{a}\begin{vmatrix} e & f \\ h & i \end{vmatrix} - \color{blue}{b}\begin{vmatrix} d & f \\ g & i \end{vmatrix} + \color{green}{c}\begin{vmatrix} d & e \\ g & h \end{vmatrix}
$$
```ts
a * (e * i - f * h)       - b * (d * i - f * g)         + c * (d * h - e * g)
```


# Cross product and 3D

Cross product takes two direction vectors, perpendicular or not, and result is new vector that is perpendicular to both of mentioned vectors.

Why is called cross product? Sign of operation resebles x.

$$
\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = 
\begin{vmatrix} 
\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 
a_x & a_y & a_z \\ 
b_x & b_y & b_z 
\end{vmatrix} 
= \hat{i} \begin{vmatrix} a_y & a_z \\ b_y & b_z \end{vmatrix} 
- \hat{j} \begin{vmatrix} a_x & a_z \\ b_x & b_z \end{vmatrix} 
+ \hat{k} \begin{vmatrix} a_x & a_y \\ b_x & b_y \end{vmatrix}

$$

$$
\Vert \vec{a} \times \vec{b} \Vert = \Vert \vec{a} \Vert \Vert \vec{b} \Vert \sin(\theta)

$$

# Getting cross product


How do we actually get cross product vector, or his components?

Well we can do it with determinante.

$$
\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = 
\begin{vmatrix} 
\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 
a_x & a_y & a_z \\ 
b_x & b_y & b_z 
\end{vmatrix} 
= \hat{i} \begin{vmatrix} a_y & a_z \\ b_y & b_z \end{vmatrix} 
- \hat{j} \begin{vmatrix} a_x & a_z \\ b_x & b_z \end{vmatrix} 
+ \hat{k} \begin{vmatrix} a_x & a_y \\ b_x & b_y \end{vmatrix}

$$

`i`, `j` `k` serve as the foundational directional arrows for 3D space, but what is important is that their value is 1 so you can omit them from multiplication, but we can keep them just to be  a marker for the component.

```ts
a ⨯ b = 
	// rember the order +, - , + for components
	+ (a.y * b.z - a.z * b.y)i
	- (a.x * b.z - a.z * b.x)j
	+ (a.x * b.y - a.y * b.x)k

a ⨯ b = 
	+ (a.y * b.z - a.z * b.y)i
	+ (a.x * b.z + a.z * b.x)j
	+ (a.x * b.y - a.y * b.x)k
```

# Magnitude of cross product and θ

What is the magnitud of direction vector we get by calculating cross product.

Well, equation is giving you that info:

$$
\Vert \vec{a} \times \vec{b} \Vert = \Vert \vec{a} \Vert \Vert \vec{b} \Vert \sin(\theta)

$$

Ok, let's look first at left side, and it clearly says that this is the magnitude of cross product vector.

Meaning we can calculate it by doing: `sqrt(x * x + y * y + z * z)`, but we need to know components of mentioned vector

Let's do it with example

Having two vectors
- a(2, 3, 4)
- b(5, 6 ,7)
let's find magnitude of cross product

```c
// we already established vector from previous example
// but let's show it again
a ⨯ b = 
	// rember the order +, - , + for components
	+ i*(3 * 7 - 4 * 6)i,
	- j*(2 * 7 - 4 * 5)j,
	+ k*(2 * 6 - 3 * 5)k
	  
a ⨯ b = 
	+ 1 * (21 - 24),
	- 1 * (14 - 20),
	+ 1 * (12 - 15)
	  

a ⨯ b = 
	+ 1 * (-3),
	- 1 * (-6),
	+ 1 * (-3)

// we found vector
a ⨯ b = (-3, 6, -3)

// now to calculate magnitude
||a ⨯ b|| = sqrt(-3 * (-3) + 6 * 6 + (-3) * (-3))

||a ⨯ b|| = sqrt(9 + 36 + 9) = sqrt(54) = 7.348469

```


Now let's look at the right side of the equation

$$
\Vert \vec{a} \times \vec{b} \Vert = \Vert \vec{a} \Vert \Vert \vec{b} \Vert \sin(\theta)

$$

We can calculate θ angle. We already have magnitude of cross product since we calculated it above.
We just need to calculate magnitude of a vector and magnitude of b vector; and we know that magnitude is length or distance, and we calculate it according to this: [[9. 2D - Distance or Magnitude#Finidng magnitude/distance/length in 3D is almost the same, we just have extra value]]

```c

||a ⨯ b|| = 7.348469   // from previous calculation

// findig magnituds of these vectors       a(2, 3, 4)   b(5, 6 ,7)

||a|| = sqrt(2*2 + 3*3 + 4*4) = sqrt(4 + 9 + 16) = sqrt(29) = 5.385
||b|| = sqrt(5*5 + 6*6 + 7*7) = sqrt(25 + 36 + 49) = sqrt(110) = 10.488

sin(θ) = ||a ⨯ b||  / (||a|| * ||b||)

sin(θ) =  7.348469 / (5.385 * 10.488) = 7.348469 / 56.48 = 0.130107458

θ = asin(0.130107458) = 0.13047735817624978 radians

θ = 0.13047735817624978 * (180/PI) = 7.4758deg
```
