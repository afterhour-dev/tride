## Points and vectors serve as building block of spatial representation in game development

- Points represent location in space
- Vector represent
	- Direction   (+-)
	- Magnitude (length)



In Game Development
- Points are used for
	- Player/enemy position
	- Target locations
	- etc
- Vectors are used for
	- Player/enemy velocity
	- Directions


## Points are also called Position Vectors
