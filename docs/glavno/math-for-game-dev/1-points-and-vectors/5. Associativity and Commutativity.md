
Direction Vector Addition is both
- Associative (Svojstvo grupisanja)
	- Grouping of vectors doesn't affect result
	- If you have 3 direction vectors, result will be the same, no matter how they're grouped
	- $$
          (\vec{a} + \vec{b}) + \vec{c} = \vec{a} + (\vec{b} + \vec{c})
	   $$
- Commutative (Svojstvo zamene redosleda)
	- Order doesn't affect the result
	- Given two direction vectors, result will be the same, no matter how they are ordered
	-  $$
          \vec{a} + \vec{b} = \vec{b} + \vec{a}
	   $$


Subtraction between Direction Vectors 
- **NOT** associative
- **NOT** commutative
