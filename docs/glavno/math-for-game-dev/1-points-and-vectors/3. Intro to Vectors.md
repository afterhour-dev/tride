## Two concepts of Positions - `Point Vectors` and `Direction Vectors`

First to tell you that offten you can see that people refer to a point, as position vector, and they refer to a vector as direction vector.

- Positions are points: often called `Position Vectors`
- Positions are also vectors: offten called `Direction Vectors`

Largly represented by same set of numbers but they have different meanings.

## Position Vectors and Direction Vectors serve as building block of spatial representation in game development


- Point or Position Vectors represent location in space
- Direction Vector, or just Vectors represent
	- Direction   (+-)
	- Magnitude (length)



In Game Development
- Points or Position Vectors are used for
	- Player/enemy position
	- Target locations
	- etc
- Vectors or Direction Vectors are used for
	- Player/enemy velocity
	- Directions
