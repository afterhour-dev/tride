# Magnitude

##        2D
$$
||\vec{v}|| = \sqrt{ a^2 + b^2}
$$

##        3D
$$
||\vec{v}|| = \sqrt{ a^2 + b^2 + c^2}
$$


# Normalization
$$\hat{v} = \frac{v}{|v|}$$
##          Normalization is multiplying by inverse of magnitude
$$\hat{v} = \frac{v}{|v|} = v \times \frac{1}{|v|}$$

# Scaling

##        by scalar -> uniform scaling

$$
\begin{gather}
a*\vec{v} = (a*v.x, a*v.y)\\
\end{gather}
$$
$$
\begin{gather}
a*\vec{v} = (a*v.x, a*v.y, a*v.z)\\
\end{gather}
$$
##        Hadamard product -> non-uniform scaling
$$
\vec{a} \circ \vec{b} = (a.x * b.x , a.y * b.y)
$$
$$
\vec{a} \circ \vec{b} = (a.x * b.x , a.y * b.y, a.z * b.z)
$$

# Dot Product (scalar product)

$$
\begin{gather}
\vec{v}_{1} \cdot \vec{v_{2}} = |\lvert a\rvert| & |\lvert b\rvert| & \cos{\theta} 
\end{gather}
$$
   left side:
$$
\begin{gather}
{v}_{1}.x * {v_{2}}.x + {v}_{1}.y * {v_{2}}.y
\end{gather}
$$


##        Scalar projection
$$\frac{v_1 \cdot v_2}{|v_2|} = \frac{|v_1||v_2|\cos\theta}{|v_2|} = |v_1|\cos\theta$$


# Cross Product (vector product)


$$
\Vert \vec{a} \times \vec{b} \Vert = \Vert \vec{a} \Vert \Vert \vec{b} \Vert \sin(\theta)
$$

$$
\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = 
\begin{vmatrix} 
\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 
a_x & a_y & a_z \\ 
b_x & b_y & b_z 
\end{vmatrix} 
= \hat{i} \begin{vmatrix} a_y & a_z \\ b_y & b_z \end{vmatrix} 
- \hat{j} \begin{vmatrix} a_x & a_z \\ b_x & b_z \end{vmatrix} 
+ \hat{k} \begin{vmatrix} a_x & a_y \\ b_x & b_y \end{vmatrix}
$$

   left side:
```ts
(ay * bz - az * by)i       -(ax * bz - az * bx)j         (ax * by - ay * bx)k
```

a × b  !==  b × a
