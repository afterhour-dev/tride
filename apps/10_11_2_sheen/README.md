# MeshPhysicalMaterial - sheen

About sheen effect of MeshPhysicalMaterial.

## Namera / Intent

Learnig about sheen effect of MeshPhysicalMaterial.

## Šta treba objasniti u detalje

- sheen
  - highlights the material when seen from narrow angle
  - usually on fluffy material like fabric
  - stronger when seen at a narrow angle
  - works best with fabric like materials and makes material feel soft
