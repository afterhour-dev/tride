# Environment map - cube map

**This app looks out of place here but it should be here since I used mesh standard material with cube map. It is comletely new app, not related to the these apps that explain mesh standard material but it should be here since I added environment map here with `HDRLoader`** Why is also maybe out of place? Becaue we are explaining drop shadows in lessons starting with 13. and this lesson uses drop shadows.

What we have here

- plane -> floor
- cube
- directional light, ambient light
- drop shadows settings enabled; you can toggle them of/on it with gui but don't need to do that

- **environmentMap texture loaded with `CubeTextureLoader`**
